# Claim status codes

| Code | Meaning | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| INTAKE | Claim received, not yet assigned | under 24 hours |
| ASSIGNED | An adjuster is assigned | 1-2 business days |
| INVESTIGATING | Adjuster is gathering evidence | 3-10 business days |
| PENDING_DOCS | Waiting on documents from the claimant | until documents arrive |
| APPROVED | Settlement approved, payment queued | payment issues in 3-5 business days |
| DENIED | Claim denied, appeal window open | appeal window is 30 days |

A claim in PENDING_DOCS for more than 14 days is auto-closed. Auto-closed claims can be reopened within 60 days without filing a new claim.

Claim status does not refresh in real time in the Claims Console. The status view refreshes every 15 minutes.

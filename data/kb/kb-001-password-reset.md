# Resetting a locked agent portal password

An agent portal account locks after five consecutive failed sign-in attempts. The lock clears automatically after 30 minutes.

To reset immediately:
1. Open the agent portal sign-in page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the registered work email address. A reset link is sent within five minutes.
3. The reset link expires 15 minutes after it is issued.
4. The new password must be at least 12 characters and cannot match the previous five passwords.

If the reset email does not arrive, confirm the address is the registered work email, not a personal address. Personal addresses are not accepted for password reset.

Escalate to Tier 2 only when the account remains locked after a successful password reset.

# Mid-term policy endorsements

An endorsement changes a policy between renewal dates. Common endorsements: adding a vehicle, changing an address, adjusting coverage limits.

Rules:
- Endorsements are effective from the date requested, not the date processed.
- Backdating is permitted up to 30 days with supervisor approval.
- Premium is recalculated pro rata for the remaining term.
- An endorsement that increases the premium by more than 25% triggers a re-underwriting review.

Endorsements cannot be applied to a policy that is in cancellation-pending status. The cancellation must be rescinded first.
